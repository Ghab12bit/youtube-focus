Unpull privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Unpull is a browser extension that changes how YouTube (and any other site you choose) looks, to reduce visual distraction. It does not collect, sell or share your data.

What is stored, and where

Everything Unpull stores stays in your own browser, using Chrome's built-in extension storage:

  • Your settings: which features are on, your preset, countdown and limit lengths, and the list of extra sites you added. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs these settings between your own devices.
  • Your goals: the text you type into the "What are you here for?" screen, and your last three goals.
  • Usage time: how many minutes you spent on YouTube per session and per week, and which days you used it. This powers the timer, the soft limit and the weekly summary.

What is never collected

Unpull has no servers, no analytics and no tracking. It makes no network requests, runs no remote code, and never sends your settings, goals, usage time or browsing to the developer or anyone else.

Permissions

  • storage: to save the settings and data listed above in your browser.
  • scripting: to apply greyscale to extra sites you add, and to update YouTube tabs that were already open when you installed it.
  • alarms: to turn Unpull back on automatically after "Pause 15m".
  • Access to youtube.com: to change how YouTube looks.
  • Optional access to other sites: only for sites you add yourself (for example x.com). Chrome asks you to allow each site, and you can remove it any time.

Deleting your data

Removing the extension deletes everything it stored. You can also click "End session" in the popup to clear the current session.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue at github.com/Ghab12bit/youtube-focus/issues.

Unpull is not affiliated with Google or YouTube.
